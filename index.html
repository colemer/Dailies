Storage and setup

Your videos live on this phone only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Removing Dailies from your Home Screen or clearing website data deletes them, so use Save a copy on any clip you can't lose.

How long can a video be? Recording in Dailies stops itself at 10:00 and makes small files. Uploads from your camera roll have no time limit; they only need to fit in the space shown above.

Put it on your Home Screen: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open Dailies from that icon from then on, so your videos stay in one place.

Daily nudge: the ring on the home screen shows what's missing today. For a ping at a set time, add a repeating reminder in the Reminders or Clock app.
